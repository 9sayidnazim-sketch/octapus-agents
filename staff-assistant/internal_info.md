## Approval Rules
- Only Sayid Nazim approves quotations, contracts, pricing, official documents, policies, and team changes.
- If a staff member asks for something outside their authority, say: "I need to confirm this with management before proceeding."
- Never assume approval or completion. Ask for confirmation and evidence.
- Billing corrections and final client deliveries need Sayid's approval before anything is sent to the client.

## Client Workflow
Create client, collect details, requirement form (always from a Hub App template, never from scratch), review, quotation, manager approval, client approval, receipt, project creation, team assignment, production. A rejected approval sets the client to NON ACTIVE.

## Completion Reports and Claims Hub
Every finished project needs a Completion Report covering client and project info, requirements, work delivered, technical details (platform, hosting, domain, repository, deployment), file storage locations, financials (quote, invoice, received, pending), and approvals. Then create the customer in Claims Hub and archive the project. Check the Mega folder before requesting any document from a client.

## Documents and Communication
- Every client has a Mega folder with: Quotes, Receipts, Contracts, Proposals, Deliverables, Assets, Meetings, Approvals, Completion Report.
- All official emails CC info@octapus.ae and gm@octapus.ae.
- PDFs sent to clients are uploaded as public links. Never send private signed links.
- Documents use Inter font, black and white, no emojis, multi-page, with department attribution.

## Escalation
Assigned person, then second reminder, then Sayid Nazim, then Shabab if Sayid is unavailable.

## Daily Routine
Morning: first reminder with the day's tasks. Afternoon: progress check. Night: completion check that requires evidence. Missed items: record the reason, carry forward, and follow up the next day. Reminders should be specific and must not repeat the same question two days in a row.
